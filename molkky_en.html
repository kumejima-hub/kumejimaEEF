🪵 How to Play Mölkky

What is Mölkky?

An outdoor game from Finland. Throw a wooden stick (the mölkky) to knock down 12 numbered pins (skittles) and try to reach exactly 50 points. Play with 2 or more people, or in teams.

1. Setting up the pins

Stand the 12 pins close together as shown below. Throw from a line about 3–4 m away from the pins.

2. How to play

Take turns, one throw each

Throw the mölkky underhand.

Count your points

See “3. Scoring” below.

Stand the pins back up where they fell

Fallen pins are stood up on the spot where they landed, so the pins spread out as the game goes on.

Next player (team)

Keep going. The first player (team) to reach exactly 50 points wins.

3. Scoring

1 pin downScore the number
on that pin
2 or more pins downScore the number
of pins knocked down

e.g. only “9” falls → 9 points / “3”, “10” and “12” fall → 3 points

Only pins lying completely on the ground count. A pin leaning on another pin or on the mölkky does not count.

4. Key rules

Exactly 50 points to win

If you go over 50, your score drops back to 25. Choose your target carefully near the end.

Three misses in a row and you’re out

A player (team) that scores 0 three times in a row is eliminated.

⚠️ Play safely

  • Make sure no one is near the pins or in the throwing direction before you throw.
  • Do not throw the mölkky overhand or toward people.
  • Please play where you will not disturb other guests.
  • After use, please return all 12 pins and the mölkky to the front desk.
