6:00–9:00
14:00–22:00

Please bring a towel from your room. Extra towels are available at the front desk.

Please leave valuables in the safe in your room.

Please do not wear the hotel loungewear or room slippers to the bath.

🔑 Card Keys

You will receive a women's bath key, a men's bath key and a guest room key. Please check which is which.

👘 Loungewear & Room Slippers

Loungewear is not provided in the guest rooms. If you would like to use it, please collect it directly from the front desk.

Please wear the loungewear and room slippers inside your guest room only.

🌿 Room Cleaning for Multi-night Stays (Eco Cleaning)

If you would like your room cleaned

By 9:00 a.m., please turn the “Please clean” magnet on the outside of your door face-up.

If you do not need cleaning

Please leave used towels and trash in the hallway. Our staff will collect them, and new towels and a ticket will be delivered in a bag hung on your door handle.

To use the ticket, bring an eligible item from the shop to the front desk to exchange it.

  • Ice cream from the shop
  • Orion happoshu (beer-like drink)
